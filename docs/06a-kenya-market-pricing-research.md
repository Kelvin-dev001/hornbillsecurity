# Kenyan Security-Systems Pricing Research — September 2026

## How to read the two price columns (important for the 40% markup rule)

Kenyan suppliers almost never publish a separate "trade" price list. What actually exists is a **tiered set of public prices for the same SKU**, and the tier tells you what it is:

| Tier | Who | Typical sites | Treat as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dealer / near-trade** | Distributors and installer-supply houses, quote **ex-VAT**, sell to trade | Alltech Kenya, Almiria Techstore, Rapidtech, Glantix (ex-VAT column), Informed Systems, TDK, Gemani | **Trade** |
| **Shop retail** | Reseller storefronts, ex-VAT but with resale margin already loaded | Techyshop / Hubtech (same stock, same phone number), Protech Line, CCTV Shop Kenya | **Retail** |
| **Consumer retail** | Marketplace, VAT-inclusive, consumer-facing | Jumia, Kilimall, Jiji | **Retail — do not mark up** |
| **Installed rate** | Contractor's own per-metre / per-point quote | electricfences.co.ke, Eclectic Fences, automaticgates.co.ke | **Already the sell price** |

**Observed dealer→shop spread on identical SKUs is 15–105%**, e.g. Nemtek Druid 15 LCD: KES 20,900 (Alltech) vs KES 43,000 (Techyshop); ZKTeco MB360: KES 24,900 (Alltech) vs KES 45,000 (Techyshop/Hubtech); Hammer EZ680: KES 27,500 (Informed) vs KES 39,000 (Techyshop). **Applying 40% to a Techyshop/Hubtech/Jumia figure will price the client above market on most lines.**

As a **turnkey package per gate**, not per component. Centurion dominates Kenya; BFT/FAAC/Came/Nice/Roger have no meaningful public Kenyan price presence — they are quote-only imports.

### Kenyan regulatory requirement — commercial premises

This is a genuine sales lever and should be on the client's site:

- Governed by the **Occupational Safety and Health Act 2007 (OSHA)** and the **Fire Risk Reduction Rules 2007 (Legal Notice No. 59)**, plus county by-laws.
- **All businesses** must hold a **Fire Safety Certificate** from the county fire department — it is a prerequisite for the Single Business Permit. Fees: **Nairobi 3,000–10,000; Mombasa 2,500–8,000; other counties 1,500–8,000**.
- **Annual fire safety audit is mandatory** under Rule 36 (documentation, physical inspection, risk assessment), carried out by a DOSH-registered fire safety auditor. Audit cost **3,000–80,000** depending on premises size.
- Non-compliance: fines up to **KES 500,000** and/or 6 months imprisonment, plus licence revocation.
- Sources: [Aquila Consolidated](https://aquilaltd.co.ke/blog/fire-safety-compliance-kenya-business-requirements-2025), [OSHA 2007 (Kenya Law)](http://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/Acts/OccupationalSafetyandHealthAct_No15of2007.pdf)
- **Extinguisher refill/recharge (recurring):** DCP 1 kg 600–900; 2 kg 900–1,300; 4 kg 1,500–2,000; 6 kg 1,800–2,500; foam 9 L 2,500–3,500; CO2 2 kg 2,200–3,000; CO2 5 kg 3,500–5,500. Hydrostatic test every 5–12 yrs. ([Bekam Orbit](https://bekamorbit.com/fire-extinguisher-refill-price-in-kenya/))

### Communications Authority of Kenya (CA) licensing

Any radio on an **assigned business frequency** (i.e. anything beyond licence-free PMR/FRS handhelds) requires a CA frequency licence:

- **Application fee: KES 1,000**
- **Frequency licence fee: KES 18,700**
- **Total: KES 19,700**, using form CA/F/FSM/03 (Fixed and Mobile Radio Communication Service)
- Processing time **74–106 days** — the frequency assignment step alone takes 70–100 days
- Source: [InvestKenya eProcedures — Pay frequency licence fee](https://eprocedures.investkenya.go.ke/procedure/print/324/216/step/0?showRecourses=true&showCertification=false&l=en&embed=true&includeSearch=false); forms at [CA Kenya](https://www.ca.go.ke/license-application-forms-fees)

**Sales note:** licence-free Baofeng/Motorola PMR sets need no licence, which is why estates and small sites buy them. Commercial guarding, logistics and estate management on assigned channels do need the licence — and the ~3-month CA lead time is a real reason to start the paperwork at order stage. Radios also require CA **type approval** to be legally sold.

### How installers quote this
Kenyan AMCs are quoted **as a flat annual fee per system**, not as a percentage of contract value, and almost always **exclude spare parts** (parts billed at the reactive rates above). The published range is startlingly low (5,000–15,000/year) because it reflects small residential systems — a 32-camera commercial site with quarterly PPM visits will be far higher. The **free site survey is a near-universal Kenyan norm**; the client should keep it free as a lead magnet and monetise the written security audit separately. Cloud recording and monitoring are the only true per-camera-per-month recurring revenue with published Kenyan rates.

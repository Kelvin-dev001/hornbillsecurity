## The rules every article follows

1. **Tables, not bullet prose.** Lance Security publishes the most price-transparent article in the Kenyan market entirely in bullets. Tables are what AI answer engines lift. This is close to a free win.
2. **Model numbers in full.** `DS-2CD1043G2-LIUF/SL`, not "a 4MP camera". Every competitor hides behind category names, which leaves an entire query class uncontested.
3. **A visible date.** "Updated September 2026" plus a machine-readable `dateModified`. Most Kenyan price pages carry no date at all, and `cctv camera price in kenya today` is a real query.
4. **Answer the question in the first 150 words**, then show the working. That opening paragraph is what gets extracted into an AI Overview.
5. **A coast angle wherever one honestly exists.** Not "Kenya" when we mean Mombasa. Local specificity is the moat.
6. **Say what does not work.** The limitations paragraph is the most trusted and most quoted part of any of these.

## The two articles that matter most, and why

**CCTV and the Law in Kenya (18 Nov).** The strongest pure content gap found in the entire research. The ODPC Draft Guidance Note for Private Security (December 2025) requires visible surveillance signage, a stated lawful basis, a justified retention period, DPIAs for high-risk processing, and subject access to footage within seven days — and essentially nobody in the industry has written about it. The only substantive treatments are on law-firm sites that do not sell installations. It is factual, dated and citable, it links naturally from every commercial page, it is the single most likely page to earn genuine editorial links, and it sells a compliance service no competitor offers.

**How to Read a CCTV Quotation (launch).** This one converts the whole strategy into an argument. A teardown of a real anonymised quote: inflated cable metres, unbranded "2MP camera" lines with no model, desktop drives sold as surveillance-grade, phantom accessory counts, labour bundled to hide it. Every competitor hiding prices becomes evidence for us. It makes the itemised BOM feel like a moral position rather than a marketing tactic, and it pre-frames every rival's "request a quote" as something to be suspicious of.

## The monthly price review

First Tuesday of each month. This is a business process, not just a post:

1. Owner updates distributor prices in the admin portal (bulk % adjust where a whole category moved).
2. `prices_updated_at` is stamped, which updates every visible "Prices updated" label and every `dateModified` on the site.
3. A short post goes out: what changed, by how much, and why. Three or four paragraphs is plenty.
4. Sitemap resubmitted; IndexNow ping.

`cctv camera price in kenya today` is a verified Kenyan query. A dated, versioned price list with a visible changelog owns freshness intent outright, and no competitor is contesting it.
